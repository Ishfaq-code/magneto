Source PDF is the authors' accepted manuscript from CNR ISTI, provided here for personal use. It retains its copyright notice.

The model follows Figure 2 and reported dimensions. It is not the authors' CAD or a manufacturing replica.
Groups: printed structure; purchased actuators; plungers/springs; offset adapters.
Electronics are documented in the paper but are outside its cell envelope and are not geometrically reconstructed.
Figure 2b labels 8 mm and 6.45 mm actuator pitches; the prose refers generally to 8 mm. The model follows the two figure pitches.
Figure 2c lid footprint is 35.4 x 25.4 mm. Case envelope is 40 x 35 x 23.8 mm.
Unknown dimensions approximated for visualization: base thickness 1.5 mm; lid thickness 2 mm; support width 3.8 mm; shaft diameter 1.7 mm; collar diameter 3.3 mm; adapter hub/beam profiles; dot diameter 1.4 mm; spring wire 0.32 mm and free height 5.4 mm; mounting holes and fastening system.
Downward movement is shown as 0.8 mm to put tips below the surface; exact downstroke is not documented. Spring shape changes are illustrative, not a force simulation.
There are six channels laid out as in Figure 2; button labels are channel IDs, not a declaration of standard Braille numbering.
