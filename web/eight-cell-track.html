Write → return → reset

Eight passive cells move over one fixed bank of six servos. Catches hold each written pattern.

At home. All eight cells blank.

Inspect motion

Forward: the hinged release flap yields to each tab. Return: its stop holds the flap upright, shifting each catch carrier to release the pins.

Size matters

Cell pitch: 50 mm (enlarged demo)
Writing span: 350 mm
Total stroke with reset margin: 400 mm
Crank radius: 225 mm
Connecting arm: 550 mm

The earlier 35 mm crank cannot cover this travel. This larger crank illustrates the geometry; its footprint is substantial.

Concept only

Catch flex, gravity return, flap contact, servo reach and clearances need physical testing. The animation assumes they work. Dimensions are illustrative, not manufacturing CAD or standard-size Braille.
